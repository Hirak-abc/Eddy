## Product Overview

Eddy is a marketing and customer-rewards platform designed for small and medium-sized businesses, particularly local businesses that depend on walk-in customers and may not have dedicated marketing teams.

It helps business owners create business-specific promotional advertisements using AI, discover relevant trends, and publish flyers and offers on social media. Unique QR codes connect online promotions with in-store customer visits through a spin wheel, discounts, and Eddy Coins.
